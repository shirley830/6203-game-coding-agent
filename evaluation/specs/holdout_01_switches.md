# Switch puzzle

Create a browser puzzle on a 4 by 4 board. Clicking a cell toggles that cell and its orthogonal neighbors, within bounds. The goal is all lights off. Generate the starting position by applying legal moves to the solved board so it is solvable. Show move count, win state, reset to the same starting board, and new puzzle. Prevent accidental double counting and show controls. No remote assets; provide one documented launch command.
